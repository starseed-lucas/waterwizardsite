About

This started because I needed an ocean for a little sailing game and couldn't find one I liked at a price I could justify, so I wrote one. It's an FFT ocean based on Tessendorf's paper, with the waves driven by wind speed, so one slider takes you from flat calm to a storm. Boats float on the actual wave height instead of a guess. The sailing game never got finished, but the water kept getting better. Everything comes with full source and the demo scene from the screenshots. If something breaks or you'd like a feature, send me an email and I'll take a look.
